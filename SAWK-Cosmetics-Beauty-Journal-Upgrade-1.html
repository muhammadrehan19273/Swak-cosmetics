SKINCARE · 4 MIN READ

A simple morning skincare routine

Keep your morning routine manageable with gentle cleansing, moisturising and daily sun protection suited to your skin.

Read the guide

Start simple

1. Cleanse gently. Use a cleanser that leaves your skin comfortable rather than tight or stripped.

2. Moisturise. Choose a moisturiser that suits your skin type and the climate you live in.

3. Use sunscreen. In the daytime, finish with broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher and reapply as directed on the label, especially outdoors.

Keep it consistent. Introduce new products one at a time and stop using anything that causes persistent irritation. For ongoing skin concerns, ask a qualified healthcare professional.

02MAKEUP
MADE EASY✦
MAKEUP · 3 MIN READ

Build an everyday makeup kit

You do not need every product. Begin with a few versatile essentials and add items only when they serve your style.

Read the guide

Your personal essentials

Prepare your base. Start with moisturised skin and choose complexion products that match your needs and tone.

Add a little definition. Mascara, brow product or a neutral eye shade can create a polished look without a complicated routine.

Choose one finishing touch. A lip colour or blush you enjoy can make the look feel complete.

Keep tools clean. Wash brushes and sponges regularly, avoid sharing eye or lip products, and follow product instructions and expiry guidance.

03SMART BEAUTY
HABITS◇
BEAUTY BASICS · 4 MIN READ

How to choose products thoughtfully

Make more confident choices by checking ingredients, your own preferences and the product's directions before buying.

Read the guide

Shop with a plan

Know your goal. Decide whether you need hydration, colour, cleansing or another specific feature before browsing.

Read the label. Check directions, ingredients, warnings and suitability information, especially if you know you are sensitive to an ingredient.

Patch test when appropriate. Follow the product's instructions and discontinue use if irritation occurs.

Give products time. Avoid changing several products at once, so it is easier to notice what suits you.

Beauty information is for general educational purposes and is not a substitute for personalised medical advice. Product results vary from person to person.
